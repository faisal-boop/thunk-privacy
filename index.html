Thunk — Privacy Policy

Last updated 7 October 2026 · Applies to Thunk: Knife Throw (com.nomadarcade.thunk on the App Store and Google Play)

The short version. You can play the whole game without an account and without giving us anything. If you choose to sign in, we store your game progress so it follows you to a new phone. Thunk has no adverts, does not track you across other apps, and we do not sell your data. The only things it sells are optional packs of in-game acorns.

Who we are

Thunk is published by Alden Morris Enterprises LLC, trading as Nomad Arcade. For anything in this policy, write to support@get-creative.co.

Playing without an account

By default the game has no account. Your progress — levels cleared, stars, acorns, knives, high scores, achievements and settings — is saved only on your device. We cannot see it, and it is deleted when you uninstall the game.

If you choose to sign in

Signing in is optional and lives in Settings → Cloud save. It exists for one reason: so your progress survives a lost or replaced phone. You can play forever without it.

WhatWhy
Your Apple ID (via Sign in with Apple) or your Google account ID (via Google Sign-In), with the name and email address that provider shares. If you choose Apple’s “Hide My Email”, we only ever see the private relay address.To recognise you when you sign in on another device
Your game progress: acorns, knives owned and selected, levels cleared and their stars, endless-mode high scores, achievements, lifetime stats and your daily-gift streak So it can be restored on a new device

Sound and haptics settings are not uploaded; they stay on each device.

Progress is stored in Google Cloud Firestore in the United States (the nam5 multi-region). Security rules restrict each record so that it can only be read or written by the signed-in account it belongs to.

Analytics and crash reports

ServiceWhat it receives
Google Analytics for FirebaseGameplay events only — a level started, cleared or failed, its number and whether it was a boss, the stars earned, an endless run's score, and which acorn pack was bought. If you are signed in, these are linked to your account identifier.
Firebase CrashlyticsCrash and error reports, with device model, operating system version and the state of the game when it crashed.

We use these to find levels that are unfairly hard and to fix crashes. Neither is used for advertising or sold to anyone. The game does not access your device’s advertising identifier.

Advertising

Thunk contains no adverts and no advertising SDK. Nothing about you is sent to an advertising network. If that changes in a future release, this policy will be updated before that version ships.

Purchases

Thunk sells optional packs of acorns, the in-game currency. Acorns are also earned by playing. Payment is handled entirely by Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play): we never see your card or payment details.

WhatWhy
The store's transaction identifier for each pack you buy, kept on your device So a purchase is credited exactly once, even if the app closes mid-purchase
An analytics event naming the pack you bought (no price or payment data) To understand which packs players find useful

Refunds are handled by Apple or Google under their own policies.

Children

Thunk is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has signed in, contact us and we will delete the account and its data.

Deleting your data

You are in control of all of it:

  • Delete your account and cloud save: Settings → Cloud save → Delete account. After you confirm with Apple or Google, this removes your stored progress and your account from our systems, and revokes the game’s Sign in with Apple authorization. The progress already on your phone is kept so you can carry on playing.
  • Sign out: Settings → Cloud save → Sign out. Nothing further is uploaded; your device copy is untouched.
  • Reset your progress: Settings → Reset all progress. While you are signed in, this resets the cloud copy too.
  • Remove everything locally: uninstall the game.
  • Ask us: email support@get-creative.co and we will delete your data.

How long we keep it

Cloud saves are kept while your account exists, and deleted when you delete it. Analytics and crash data are retained by Google for up to 14 months and then removed automatically.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, export or delete the data we hold, and to object to how we use it. Email support@get-creative.co and we will respond within 30 days. You may also complain to your local data protection authority.

Who else sees your data

We do not sell your data and we do not share it for advertising. It is processed on our behalf by Google (Firebase Authentication, Cloud Firestore, Analytics, Crashlytics) and, if you use it, by Apple for Sign in with Apple. Those companies are bound by their own privacy terms.

Changes

If this policy changes in a way that affects what we collect, we will update the date at the top and, where the change is significant, tell you in the game.
